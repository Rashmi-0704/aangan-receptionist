# What makes a qualified lead (PLACEHOLDER)

> Replace this file with the real criteria. It is used by the voice agent (what to ask)
> and by the Gemini classifier (how to tag the call).

A lead is **qualified** when ALL of these are true:
1. The project is in an area we serve (Pune / PCMC).
2. It is a full-home, kitchen/wardrobe or office interior project (not a single piece of furniture or repair).
3. Size is at least 500 sq ft, or it is a complete kitchen.
4. Budget is ₹5 lakh or more (or the caller is open to a designer's estimate).
5. They plan to start within the next 6 months.

A lead is **unqualified** when any of these apply:
- Outside our service area
- Job seekers, vendors, sales calls, wrong numbers
- Very small jobs (single item, repair, painting only)
- Timeline more than 12 months away with no firm plans

If the information is incomplete but the lead looks promising, mark it qualified and say what is missing in the reason.
